2. Free, and the best next step

  • Hernán MA, Robins JM. Causal Inference: What If. Boca Raton: Chapman & Hall/CRC; 2020. Free online from the authors. Part I needs no maths beyond proportions.
  • Hernán MA. Causal Diagrams: Draw Your Assumptions Before Your Conclusions. HarvardX online course (edX). Free, short videos, many examples. The natural follow-on to today.
  • DAGitty (dagitty.net). Draw diagrams in the browser and get adjustment sets. The R package of the same name does this inside your analysis.
  • Pearl J, Mackenzie D. The Book of Why. New York: Basic Books; 2018. A readable history of causal thinking, with the birth weight paradox and Simpson's paradox.
