Cooking Fuel and ARI

Draw a causal diagram for a published NFHS-4 analysis, find an adjustment set, and test the authors' model. Seven steps.

Mondal and Paul studied 247,743 children under five in NFHS-4 (2015-16). ARI: cough with short, rapid or difficult breathing in the last two weeks, reported by the mother. Adjusted odds ratio for biomass cooking fuel: 1.10 (1.01 to 1.20).

Mondal D, Paul P. PLoS ONE 2020;15(8):e0237611.
