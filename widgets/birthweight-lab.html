Why small babies of smokers do better

A low birth weight baby has to be small for some reason. If the mother smoked, smoking is often the reason, and it is a mild one. If she did not smoke, the reason is more often a birth defect or another serious cause. Restricting to low birth weight compares babies who are small for different reasons.
