The chain: mediators

When the exposure works through a middle variable, adjusting for that variable removes the effect you wanted.

By the end of this module you can

  1. Recognise a chain and name the mediator
  2. Calculate what adjusting for a mediator does to a risk ratio
  3. Decide when not to adjust for a variable measured after the exposure
