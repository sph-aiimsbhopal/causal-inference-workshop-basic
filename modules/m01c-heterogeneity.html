Effects that differ

The same treatment can help some people far more than others, and whether it does depends on the scale.

By the end of this module you can

  1. Tell a different baseline risk from a different effect
  2. Show that one risk ratio can mean very different absolute benefits
  3. Say which scale a claim of heterogeneity is on
