The fork: confounding

A common cause can make two things look linked when neither causes the other. Learn to spot it and remove it.

By the end of this module you can

  1. Recognise a fork and the open back-door path it creates
  2. Remove confounding by stratifying, and calculate stratum risk ratios
  3. Calculate a standardised risk ratio by hand
