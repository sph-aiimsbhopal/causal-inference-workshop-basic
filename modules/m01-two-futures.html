Two possible futures

What we mean when we say smoking causes TB death: a comparison of two futures for the same people.

By the end of this module you can

  1. Describe a causal effect as a comparison of two potential outcomes
  2. Explain why we can never see both outcomes for one person
  3. Calculate a causal risk difference and risk ratio from a table of potential outcomes
