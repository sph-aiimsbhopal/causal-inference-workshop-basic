# Causal Inference in Epidemiology: A Basic Workshop

A three-hour pre-conference workshop for MD students in Community Medicine on causal inference in observational epidemiology: potential outcomes, causal diagrams (DAGs), the structure of confounding and selection bias, and choosing an adjustment set.

Every exercise uses a published study: the Nurses' Health Study and Women's Health Initiative on hormone therapy, the Tamil Nadu case-control study of smoking and tuberculosis deaths, the birth weight paradox, and an NFHS-4 analysis of cooking fuel and childhood acute respiratory infection. Rothman's *Modern Epidemiology* (4th edition) is the anchor text.

The site contains the slide deck, a before and after quiz, four interactive labs for phones, a printable exercise workbook, and a further-learning page.
